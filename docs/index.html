SkyrimNet Relationships · how it works

Two ladders, not one

Romantasy scores what you did. This scores how you behaved — and every companion judges it for themselves, because no two of them want the same things.

01  /  ENROLL

They have to still be there

Anyone who becomes a follower starts being watched. Nothing happens for two game hours — long enough that a mercenary hired and dismissed in the same breath never enters at all.

02  /  AUTHOR

Somebody writes who they are

On enrollment a language model reads their actual bio and diary and writes a character: who they could fall for, what closeness asks of them, how much of what they feel shows, and what they ask of you about other partners.

Also
one line on how they hold it, one they will not cross
Likes
up to 7, from 58 tracked pursuits
Dislikes
up to 4, from the same list
03  /  JUDGE

Conversation is read in the background

A quiet judge reads what actually passed between you and decides whether anything shifted. Most exchanges move nothing. A flicker — a pretext to keep them around, a compliment that lands — is worth a fiftieth of a stage.

Range
a flicker to a landmark, and downward too
Landmark
350 — deciding to be something new
Never
the same moment paid twice
